## Issues, ranked

No correctness defects in the shipped batch. The two items below are **balance/consistency
consequences** of HITBOX1 + the standing range ruling that the owner has now decided (Dev Notes
1 & 2) — they are data/geometry retunes, not defects.

### 1. [Medium — balance, owner-decided] HITBOX1 enlarged every area footprint at fixed cost
The AR hitbox rule is net more generous than centre-in: the roster's four `range: 2` cones go
4→8 tiles, radius-1 circles 5→9, radius-2 13→21, radius-3 25→37 (lines unchanged). Damage was
tuned against the **old** footprints, so every circle/cone is now stronger at the same cost.
**Owner ruling (Dev Note 1): tune the ranges DOWN** so the post-HITBOX1 footprint ≈ the old
one. This is **Designer/data**, not engine. **Fix: backlog HITBOX-tune.** Ruled in edge-cases.

### 2. [Medium — geometry consistency, owner-decided] A rotated cone covers more than axis-aligned
The standing MET1×AIM2 ruling meters directional range as a *tile count* along the axis, so a
45° cone is √2 longer in real distance and its area grows with the square — `range: 2` is 8
tiles axis-aligned vs 12 at 45°. **Owner ruling (Dev Note 2): option (b)** — meter the cone's
wedge **half-width in Euclidean tiles** (an integer perpendicular-distance ramp, no trig), which
makes the area **near-rotation-invariant**. This supersedes the *cone* half of the tile-count
ruling; `line` (degenerate zero-width wedge) is unchanged. **Fix: backlog CONE-B** (engine,
ships with a rotation-invariance test). Ruled in edge-cases.

### 3. [Low — reads-differently, owner-decided] Fog hides the enemy team on the opening frame
Spawn separation 13 vs sight 6 means each team sees an empty half-board at match start. **Owner
ruling (Dev Note 3): intended — fog the opening frame too, no turn-1 grace reveal.** The
opposing team should not be visible on the very first render. **Fix: backlog VISION1-opening**
(client — apply team vision to the initial frame). Ruled in edge-cases.
