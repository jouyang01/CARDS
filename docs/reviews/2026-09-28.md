## 1. Dev Note #1 — review the TTK package (PR #102) for the Builder to implement

**The TTK spec is sound and ready to implement.** I re-checked its spine against the roster on `main`:

- **The diagnosis is correct and specific.** Sustained TTK is only ~20% fast (a nicety); the real fault
  is **burst in the 2v2 default** — two ults deal exactly 85 to Wisp's 85 HP, a kill from full with no
  error. 4v4 already matches AR. That is precisely the owner's stated concern ("one error → instant
  death"), correctly located.
- **The HP lever is the right instrument, and it replaces the ult cap.** The problem is a ratio and HP is
  its denominator; raising the bars fixes it at the source and restores the archetype ladder (supports
  1.26–1.58× firepower, near AR's 1.33–1.65×) instead of shaving five ult numbers. At the new bars the
  double-ult lands at 85% of the squishiest vs AR's 83%. **No ult is nerfed — the batch says so, so a
  Builder does not apply both fixes.** ✔
- **The tiered 1.25× skill table is load-bearing, not decoration.** A *flat* 1.25× would raise sustained
  output ~10% and fight the HP gain; the two ordering rules (single-target > area, clean > ridered) plus
  the added displacement class pull the roster back so median output lands at 22.0/turn — exactly today's.
  The eleven integers are final and tabulated. **The one call to watch is Kestrel's Skim 12→30** (the sole
  ceiling case, 86% of her ult; fallback 26 is flagged). ✔
- **The turn limit and Lumen's heal are correctly marked must-ship-together.** HP without `TURN_LIMIT` 20
  ends every 2v2 on the clock (19.7 turns for 4 kills vs a 16 limit); HP without Lumen 25→20 makes a
  healer comp outlast the match (heals don't scale with the bar). Both are arithmetic, both verified. ✔

**Verdict: implement the package as specced.** Dev Note #1 is the owner directing implementation, which
also answers the review's open sequencing question (§7): the five items move to the active queue, ahead of
the playtest — a playtest of numbers we already intend to change spends real humans on values that won't
ship. The five items keep their AC/Spec Notes; I have re-stated them in the backlog and marked the whole
batch UNBLOCKED. **One flag carried to the owner unchanged:** Skim at 30 vs 26, and `KILLS_TO_WIN`
staying 4 (recommended) — neither blocks the Builder.

## 2. Dev Notes #2 & #3 — the two just-shipped abilities

### Dev Note #2 — Warding Wall "not a trap that goes away after being hit" → WALL-HIT-ONCE

I traced every layer and the engine is **correct**: a tile is consumed on trigger (`resolve.ts:774`), the
wall expires end of its placement turn (lifetime 1 → `expiresOnTurn = turn`), playback deletes on
`trapTriggered`/`trapExpired`, and the renderer rebuilds its trap layer each `show()`. So this is not a
consumption/expiry/render bug. It is the **`perTile` nature**: a wall is four independent one-shot traps,
so one unit can be hit several times by the same wall (walking its length = 75, per the Builder's own
note). **The owner disambiguated (2026-09-28): keep it a multi-target barrier, but a single unit is hit at
most once.** **Fix: backlog WALL-HIT-ONCE (engine)** — a per-unit-per-cast dedup (a group id on the wall's
tiles + a per-turn `unitId × groupId` set), **not** whole-wall consumption. Ruled PROPOSED in edge-cases.

### Dev Note #3 — "Ram Charge line attack preview is not working, just go back to how it was before" → RAM-PREVIEW-REVERT

The owner has rejected RAM-LINE-PREVIEW-FIX and this **answers the Builder's OQ #1** (which asked whether
the fix satisfied the note): it did not. **Fix: backlog RAM-PREVIEW-REVERT (client)** — revert the
**lane outline** (the visible change): `tessellate`'s `path` case back to `return []`, remove the route
from the boundary memo key, and drop the app.ts path-outline draw. **Keep** the `chargeHits`
number-correctness fix (reverting it restores a preview that *lies* — Skim showing damage on enemies it
cannot hit — a bug, not a feature; and it is invisible to Ram Charge, which is `chargeHits:"all"`) and the
engine `chargedUnits`/`chargeVictims` export (harmless single-source-of-truth the number fix depends on).
One-line flag to the owner: first-only charges keep the corrected single-number preview.
