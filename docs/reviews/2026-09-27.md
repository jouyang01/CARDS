ability descriptions. "Basic damage" is the primary single-target value; conditional maxima (Tol-Ren's

### 1.3 Healing and shielding — per-cast is fine, throughput is not

Per **cast**, we are already in AR's band: Bastion's Bulwark 30 on a 130 bar is 23% against AR's ~20%
median shield; Warding Halo's 40 on an ally is 40%, exactly Helio's Battleforged 60-on-150. The problem
is **sustained throughput** under one-ability-per-turn:

| | Unit | Bar | Per turn | % of a bar/turn |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AR | Helio — Blast Shield 30 × 2 charges (cd3) | 160 | 20.0 | 12.5% |
| AR | Khita — Take Aim 25 (cd4) + Resonance Burst 30 (cd3) | 130 | 16.2 | 12.5% |
| AR | Dr. Finn — Refreshing Spray 27 (cd3) + Bubble Trouble 45 (cd4) | 165 | 20.2 | 12.3% |
| AR | Aurora — Healing Flare 30 (cd3), basic heals 10 | 160 | 16.7 | 10.4% |
| | **AR median / max** | | | **9.7% / 12.5%** |
| CARDS | **Lumen** — Mending Light 25 (cd2) alternating with basic heal 12 | 105 | **18.5** | **17.6%** |
| CARDS | Thorn — Verdant Veil 20 (cd2) | 100 | 10.0 | 10.0% |
| CARDS | Aegis — Barrier Pulse 20 (cd2) | 120 | 10.0 | 8.3% |
| CARDS | Bastion — Bulwark 30 self (cd3) | 130 | 10.0 | 7.7% |
| | **CARDS median / max** | | | **8.3% / 17.6%** |

Lumen is the outlier at **1.4× AR's best support**, and structurally so: Radiant Lash heals 12 *while*
dealing 14, so Mending Light's 2-turn cooldown never leaves a gap. She is never off-duty.

### 1.4 The one-error ladder — where "instant death" actually lives

A full-HP squishy caught in the open, focused by two attackers (CARDS 2v2 default; AR shown at the same
two-attacker count for a like-for-like comparison):

| Scenario | CARDS today (Wisp, 85 HP) | AR (120 HP) |
|---|---|---|
| 2 basics | 58 → 27 left (32%) | 70 → 50 left (42%) |
| 1 ult + 1 basic | 77 → 8 left (9%) | 86 → 34 left (28%) |
| **2 ults** | 85 → **DEAD from full** | 100 → 20 left (17%) |
| 2 ults, behind cover | 43 left | 70 left |

**This is the owner's concern, located.** Sustained TTK is only ~20% fast — a tuning nicety. The failure
is burst: two ults deal exactly 85 to an 85 HP bar. No error is required beyond standing in line of
sight. Our **4v4** is fine and matches AR (four basics = 122% of the squishiest vs AR's 115% — one-turn
focus kills were AR's design too); the fault is specific to the default 2v2.

### 2.2 Skill damage (directive #2)

> **Rule.** `new damage = round_half_up( basic × shape × rider × delay )`, where **basic** is the
> character's `abilities[0]` headline `damage` amount (conditional bonuses such as `axisBonus` and
> `innerBonus` excluded), and:
>
> | Factor | Value | Applies when |
> |---|---|---|
> | **shape** | **1.25** single-target | no `radius`, no `impact`, no `beamWidth`; shape `line` or `path` |
> | | **1.00** area | any of `radius`, `impact.destination`, `beamWidth`, or shape `circle` / `cone` |
> | **rider** | **1.00** none | the ability applies no enemy-facing non-damage effect |
> | | **0.88** status | applies `slow`, `weaken`, `root`, `reveal`, or `damageOverTime` |
> | | **0.76** displacement | applies `knockback` or `pull` |
> | **delay** | **1.25** | `delayTurns ≥ 1` — the hit is telegraphed and dodgeable |

The ceiling is **1.25× basic**, reached by a single-target skill with no rider, exactly as directed.
Single-target always beats area at equal rider class (1.25 > 1.00; 1.10 > 0.88), and no-rider always
beats ridered at equal shape (1.25 > 1.10; 1.00 > 0.88) — both owner rules hold by construction.
**Displacement is its own rider class, below status**, because `roster-v1.md` §4 calls displacement
"the strongest soft-CC in the game" (it cancels the victim's Move).

**The eleven damaging skills. These integers are final — the Builder writes them, does not recompute
them.** The formula is recorded for the *next* character, not for re-deriving this table.

### 2.3 `TURN_LIMIT` 2v2: 16 → 20 (directive #3)

Not cosmetic — **without it the whole package fails.** At two attackers and the 60% hit rate
`roster-v1.md` §4 assumes:

| | Turns per kill | Turns for `KILLS_TO_WIN` | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| CARDS 2v2 today | 3.8 | **15.2** for 4 kills | 16 — already at the edge |
| CARDS 2v2 after the HP raise | 4.9 | **19.7** for 4 kills | 16 ❌ → **20 ✓** |
| AR 4v4 (reference) | 2.4 | 12.0 for 5 kills | 20 ✓ |

AR had slack because 4v4 supplies **four** attackers per kill target; our default supplies **two**. Raise
the bars 30% without raising the limit and every 2v2 ends on the clock rather than on kills.

### 2.4 Lumen's Mending Light 25 → 20 (directive #4)

Also not cosmetic. Heals are absolute numbers; they do not scale with a bigger bar, so raising HP makes
the healer grind **worse** in wall-clock turns:

| | Net damage/turn vs Lumen | Turns for one kill |
|---|---|---|
| Today | 7.9 | 12.7 |
| HP raised, Mending Light **25** | 7.9 | **16.5 — longer than the match** |
| HP raised, Mending Light **20** | 10.4 | **12.5 ✓** |

At 20, Lumen's sustained throughput becomes 16.0/turn on a 140 bar = **11.4% of a bar/turn**, landing
inside AR's support band (median 9.7%, max 12.5%) for the first time. **The amount changes, not the
cooldown** — Mending Light stays at `cooldown: 2`, so directive #3 of the 2026-09-23 review ("Prep
cooldowns are correct right now") is untouched.

## 3. Second-order checks the Builder does not need to re-derive

### 3.1 The two levers cancel, which is why 1.25× is safe

Simulating greedy damage-max play (one ability per turn, take the biggest hit available) over a 20-turn
match at the shipped CD-BAND cooldowns:

| Skill damage set to… | Median sustained output |
|---|---|
| Today's values | **22.0**/turn |
| Parity with each character's basic | 22.0/turn (±0) |
| Flat 1.25× basic, no ordering rules | ~24/turn (+8–12%) |
| **The tiered table in §2.2** | **22.0/turn (±0)** |

**The owner's two ordering rules are what make 1.25× safe.** A flat 1.25× would raise output ~10% and
claw back part of the HP gain; pulling area and ridered skills back below the ceiling lands median output
exactly where it is today. Per character: Cinder +25% (a 1.25 ceiling off the roster's smallest basic,
14), Kestrel +8%, Vex +7%, everyone else ≤ +3%. **Do not "simplify" the tiers into a flat multiplier —
the tiers are the reason the TTK arithmetic works.**

### 3.2 Where the package lands

| Metric | Today | **After** | Atlas Reactor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Median HP | 100 | **130** | 150 |
| Basic as % of a bar | 22.0% | **16.9%** | 17.3% |
| TTK (turns of connected fire) | 4.5 | **5.9** | 5.8 |
| Skill ÷ basic | 0.64 | **1.07** | 0.96 |
| Ult as % of a bar | 38% | **29%** | 23% |
| 2 ults vs the squishiest | **100% — lethal** | **85%** | 83% |
| 2 best non-ult hits vs the squishiest | 68% | **63%** | — |
| Best support throughput | 17.6%/turn | **11.4%/turn** | 9.7–12.5%/turn |
| Turns for the kill target | 15.2 / 16 | **19.7 / 20** | 12.0 / 20 |

**The owner's goal is met:** one error costs a squishy most of a bar and leaves them a decision, instead
of being a coin-flip death.

### 3.3 Shields, catalysts and the ult clock need no compensating change

- **Shields land in AR's band by themselves** once the bars grow: Bulwark 30 on 170 = 18% (AR ~20%),
  Barrier Pulse 20 on a 100 HP ally = 20%, Warding Halo 40 = 40% (Helio's Battleforged: 40%), Fortress
  Protocol 50 on 170 = 29%. **No shield value changes in this batch.**
- **Catalysts get relatively weaker and that is correct** — Second Wind 30 on Wisp is 30% of a bar (was
  35%), Ablative Field 40 is 40% (was 47%). Both remain the strongest single answers to a predicted
  burst, which is their job. **No catalyst value changes.**
- **The ult clock is untouched.** No `energyGain` changes; `ULT_COST` and `PASSIVE_ENERGY` unchanged.
  Longer matches mean ults are *available* for more turns, which is the point of raising the limit, not
  a side effect to correct. `roster-v1.md` §4's "ults online turns 8–10" still holds.
- **`KILLS_TO_WIN` stays 4** for 2v2. The turn limit is the lever; changing both would over-correct.

## 4. Judgement calls, flagged

1. **Displacement as its own rider class (0.76).** The owner named two rules; this is a third I added,
   and it is the reason Ram Charge and Chain Hook sit at 23 rather than 26. Justification is
   `roster-v1.md` §4's own displacement budget. Drop it and both go to 26.
2. **Traps excluded from the multiplier.** They are conditional damage with no term in the formula.
   The alternative — treating a trap as area/none and multiplying — would put Warding Wall at 25 → 25
   (no change) and Overwatch Trap at 26 → 26, so excluding them costs almost nothing today, but it is a
   stated choice, not an oversight.
3. **Kestrel's Skim 12 → 30 is the biggest single jump in the batch** and the only ability that reaches
   the 1.25 ceiling. It also crowds her ultimate: Tempest Run is 35, so Skim would be 86% of an ult on a
   4-turn cooldown. AR's comparable damage-dash (Kaigin's Void Strike, 32 on cd5) sat at 64% of his ult.
   **Fallback if it plays badly: 26** (the single-target/status tier). Flagged for the playtest.
4. **Bastion's Chain Hook 10 → 23** more than doubles, and it is a range-5 pull-2. AR's near-exact
   analogue is Rampart's Fusion Lance — 25 damage plus a pull, cd3 — so the number is AR-normal, but
   this is the roster's only pull ≥ 2 and it is now a real threat rather than a utility poke.
5. **Bastion's two skills both land on 23.** A consequence of the formula, not a mistake. Ram Charge is a
   dash on cd4 and Chain Hook a ranged blast on cd3, so they are not interchangeable in play.
6. **The greedy model understates how often skills get cast.** It picks by raw damage, so it never
   accounts for Ravok preferring Shockwave (19, no recoil) over Whirling Cleave (22, minus 11 to
   himself), or for anyone casting a skill to land a slow. Real skill usage will be higher than modelled,
   which makes §3.1's "±0" a conservative floor, not a ceiling.

## 5. Sequencing — this is Path A's tuning pass, and it should land before the playtest

BACKLOG's Path A puts **PLAYTEST** next after the two client bug fixes, with its stated output being
"a short list of felt problems → a tuning pass, mostly data (numbers), not engine." **This package is
that tuning pass, arriving early because the owner directed it from measurement rather than from felt
play.**

Recommended order: **WALL-CAST-FIX → RAM-LINE-PREVIEW-FIX → the five TTK items → PLAYTEST.** The
argument is that a playtest of numbers you already intend to change spends the scarcest resource in the
project — real humans playing — on values that will not ship. The two bug fixes still go first and still
gate everything: an ability that cannot be cast cannot be tuned.

**This is the owner's call and the one thing in this review that is not a measurement.** If the
preference is to playtest the current numbers first and fold this package into the tuning pass
afterwards, the five items keep their specs unchanged and simply move behind PLAYTEST.

## 7. Open questions for the owner

1. **Sequencing (§5) — before or after the playtest?** The only genuinely open decision here.
2. **Kestrel's Skim at 30 (§4.3).** It is the ceiling case and it crowds her ult. Ship at 30 and watch,
   or start at 26?

   limit and would keep matches at ~15 turns. I recommend the turn limit (a longer match shows more of
   the kit; a shorter kill target shows less), but both solve the pacing failure and only one is needed.
