## Issues, ranked

### 1. [Low — flag] Ally pass-through: planner accepts paths resolution won't complete
`movement.ts` marks ally squares walk-through (`canStop:false`) so a path may be *planned*
through a stationary ally, but `resolve.stepMovers` correctly halts a mover in front of a
stationary ally (the no-two-units-per-square invariant). So a drawn path can silently
under-deliver. Ruled acceptable for v1 (edge-cases) — but the **targeting UI (item 18)
must reflect it** (show walk-through squares as non-terminal and not promise the full
path), or a later item adds true same-turn slide-through. No engine change needed now.

engine actually ships.
