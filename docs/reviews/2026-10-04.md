## One observation (not a bug)

`selectUnit` (`app.ts:1218`) does not itself re-frame — the camera follows the selected character on the
next `fitCamera` trigger (boot, resize, projection/orbit toggle), so **switching between your two
characters during planning may not recentre the camera on the newly selected one.** CAMERA-CONTROLS' AC is
met (the planning frame targets the selected character), and forcing a snap on every character switch could
feel jarring, so I am **not** speccing a change — flagging it so, if the playtest wants the camera to follow
character selection, it is a one-line `fitCamera()` in `selectUnit` and a known lever.
