## Issues, ranked

### 1. [Medium — correctness, shipped bug] Move phase lets two units swap squares (pass-through)
`packages/engine/src/movement.ts` `resolveMovePhase`. The same-step logic catches two
units entering the *same* square, but not two units exchanging squares across a shared
edge. Verified empirically this session:
- u0 `(0,0)→(1,0)→(2,0)`, u1 `(3,0)→(2,0)→(1,0)` → **u0 ends (2,0), u1 ends (1,0)** —
  they passed through each other on step 2.

Both paths validate (each targets only squares empty at turn start), so validation can't
catch it; the resolver must. This violates edge-cases "Units never pass through the enemy
unit." **Fix (BACKLOG item 3a):** in the fix-point, treat a same-step square *exchange*
as a contest — when two moving units each target the other's current square this step,
both stop. Generalise to any one-step cycle that permutes occupied squares (N-unit safe).
Ruled in `edge-cases.md`; regression test = the repro above.

### 2. [Medium — coverage gap, blocks item 12] No shape→squares expansion; Blast under-hits real characters
`resolveBlastDamage` (resolve.ts) treats an ability's *raw aimed target squares* as its
hit-set, so `line`/`cone`/`circle` are not spread across their footprint. Vex's Rail Shot
(a `line`, range 8) currently damages only the exact squares in `order.target`, not the
line it should sweep. This is a genuine backlog gap — there is **no item** for shape
expansion, and item 12 (full Vex-vs-Bastion match) needs it. Flagged by the Builder in
`resolveBlastDamage` and DECISIONS. **Fix:** new **BACKLOG item 5a** (shape expansion),
sequenced before item 12; wall occlusion for line/cone lands there too (ruled in
edge-cases). Until then the interim contract is acceptable and documented.

### 3. [Low — self-buff energy deferred] Self-buff-on-use energy not yet granted
GAME_SPEC §4: "self-buffs grant their listed energy on use." Only on-hit energy (damaging
Blast) is implemented; a prep self-buff like Bastion's Bulwark (`energyGain: 6`) currently
banks nothing. Correct seam for now — self-buffs don't *do* anything until effects apply
(item 6). **Fix:** fold self-buff-on-use energy into item 6 (status/effect application),
called out in that item's Spec Notes.

### 4. [Low — resolved this session] ARCHITECTURE.md signature was stale
ARCHITECTURE.md showed the 3-arg `resolveTurn`; the shipped signature is 4-arg with
`roster`. The Builder correctly flagged it (couldn't edit it under its role boundary).
Updated ARCHITECTURE.md this session to the real signature with rationale, and added the
project-direction framing (Dev Note 1). Closes Builder OQ1.

### 5. [Low — preventive, carried from last review] CI determinism guard still narrow
`.github/workflows/ci.yml` greps only `Math.random|Date.now|new Date(`; golden rule #1
also forbids float game-math and `performance.now`. Code is clean today; widen the guard
in item 11. (Unchanged from 2026-08-12; still open.)
