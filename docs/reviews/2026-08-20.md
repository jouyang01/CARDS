ending on its square. Ruled: a Dash ending on the decoy should also destroy it (both are the
enemy voluntarily occupying the ghost's square); involuntary knockback/pull does **not**
(mirrors "knockback doesn't trigger traps"). **Fix: backlog D1-dash** (small).

real fix is a Designer/data change (remove one Thorn ability, add a dash), after which the
guardrail tightens back to all kits. Ruled in edge-cases; **backlog Thorn-dash**.
