## CHASE1 — the four edge cases, RULED (the item that was blocked on me)

Folded into edge-cases ("Chase orders") so the Builder is unblocked:

1. **Chase a target you cannot see → last-known square, stop there (owner ruling).** *"You cannot
   chase a target you cannot see; you will go to their last known square if possible, but not chase
   it past where you lost vision."* This **overrides the Designer's "lean: legal"** — pathing to a
   target's true fogged position would leak hidden information (golden rule #5). So: if the chaser's
   team sees the target at resolution, chase its actual square; else chase the **team's last-known
   square** for it and **stop** (no pursuit past it); never-seen → the chase is dropped. This
   requires **engine-side per-team last-known state** (LAST-KNOWN shipped client-only; chase
   resolution is engine logic, so the engine needs its own authoritative record) — defined
   deterministically at turn granularity in the ruling.
2. **Chase vs chase** — both resolve against the same frozen post-Move snapshot; they converge, not
   stalemate. 3. **Dead target** — chase dropped, unit holds. 4. **Chase + dash** — the dash is the
   movement, chase dropped. All ruled in edge-cases; determinism + N-unit safety spelled out.
