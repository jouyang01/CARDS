## 1. The evidence — every Atlas Reactor cooldown, measured

The directive was prompted by the observation that *"most AR abilities have ≥3 turn cooldowns and
CARDS has a lot of 2 turn cooldowns."* That is correct, and the gap is larger than it looks.

Source: the community build-planner [`wiskerz/ar-builds`](https://github.com/wiskerz/ar-builds), whose
`site/index.html` embeds a full scrape of the Atlas Reactor wiki — 31 Freelancers × 5 abilities with
phase and cooldown, at end-of-life patch state. Parsed rather than recalled. **AR's kit structure is
identical to ours**: slot 1 is a 0-cooldown Blast for all 31 lancers, slots 2–4 are the cooldown
skills, slot 5 is the energy ultimate. So the comparison is one-to-one against our
`abilities[0]` + 3 skills + `ultimate`.

### 1.1 AR's 93 skill cooldowns

`0:3 · 1:2 · 2:5 · 3:25 · 4:35 · 5:16 · 6:4 · 7:2 · 8:1` — **mean 3.80, median 4, 89% are ≥3.**
A 2-turn skill is genuinely rare in Atlas Reactor: **5 of 93**.

### 1.2 By phase — AR vs. CARDS today

| Phase | Game | n | Mean | Median | Distribution | ≥3 turns |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Prep** | Atlas Reactor | 43 | 3.47 | 4 | 0:3 · 2:3 · 3:14 · 4:17 · 5:5 · 8:1 | 86% |
| | **CARDS** | 10 | 3.10 | 3 | 2:3 · 3:4 · 4:2 · 5:1 | 70% |
| **Dash** | Atlas Reactor | 21 | 4.81 | 5 | 2:1 · 4:7 · 5:9 · 6:2 · 7:2 | 95% |
| | **CARDS** | 9 | 2.67 | 3 | 2:3 · 3:6 | 67% |
| **Blast** (non-basic) | Atlas Reactor | 29 | 3.55 | 4 | 1:2 · 2:1 · 3:11 · 4:11 · 5:2 · 6:2 | 90% |
| | **CARDS** | 8 | 2.25 | 2 | 2:6 · 3:2 | 25% |
| **All skills** | Atlas Reactor | 93 | 3.80 | 4 | — | 89% |
| | **CARDS** | 27 | 2.70 | 3 | — | 56% |

**The owner's read is confirmed on all three points.** Prep is already close to AR (3.10 vs 3.47) —
hence directive #3. Dash is the outlier: our median is **3** against AR's **5**, and *no* CARDS dash
reaches AR's modal value. Non-basic Blast is second: six of our eight sit at 2, a value AR used once
in 29.

### 1.3 Why AR priced dashes highest — the commitment gradient

AR's dash numbers are not flat; they track **which direction the dash points**. Dashes carrying an
enemy-facing payload — the frontliner's engage — sit at **4** (Asana Rebounding Charge, Garrison Heavy
Metal, Titus Berserker Charge, Rask Pain Train, Phaedra Juggernaut, Tol-Ren Rushing Steel). Dashes
that are pure repositioning *away* climb to **5–7** (Blackburn Blitz 5, Grey Slip Away 6, Quark
Quantum Collision 6, Lockwood Backup Plan 7, Gremolitions Bombing Run 7). The principle: **planting
yourself in front of the enemy is its own price; getting out is not, so it must be paid for in
cooldown.** This is the rule §2 applies, and it is why the recommendation is not a flat "+2 to every
dash".

## 2. Recommended numbers

Both bands land **entirely inside the ranges the owner named** — every dash is 4 or 5, every non-basic
blast is 3 or 4. The "exceptions" below are deviations from *my own sub-rule*, not from the directive.

### 2.1 Dash — the commitment rule

> **A dash with an enemy-facing effect (damage / knockback / root) costs 4. A dash with no
> enemy-facing effect — pure teleport, or a self/ally shield — costs 5.**

A self-shield is escape insurance, not commitment, so it does not buy the discount.

| Character | Dash | Payload | Now | **New** | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bastion | Ram Charge | damage 15 + knockback 1 | 3 | **4** | Enemy-facing engage; lands you in the fight. |
| Ravok | Bullrush | damage 14 + knockback 1 | 3 | **4** | Same; Ravok's whole kit is already melee-committed. |
| Kestrel | Skim | damage 12 | 2 | **4** | The one Firepower dash with a payload — it earns the engage price, not the escape price. |
| Thorn | Bramble Stride | damage 10 + root 1 | 3 | **4** | Root is enemy-facing; the dash commits. |
| Aegis | Intercept | teleport + shield 12 | 3 | **5** | No enemy-facing effect. **Barrier Pulse (Prep 2, shield 20, r4) remains the every-other-turn bodyguard button** — Intercept becomes the expensive repositioning save. AR analogue: Brynn's Flight of the Valkyrie, 5. |
| Cinder | Backdraft | teleport | 3 | **5** | Pure disengage. |
| Lumen | Glimmer Step | teleport + shield 15 | 3 | **5** | Self-shield ≠ commitment; this is the healer's escape. AR analogues: Khita Vaulting Shot 5, Su-Ren Shifting Winds 5. |
| Vex | Combat Roll | teleport | 2 | **5** | Pure disengage on the longest-range Firepower (r8 basic). AR analogue: Celeste On the Run, 5. |
| Wisp | Blink | teleport | 2 | **4** | **EXCEPTION** — see §2.2. |

Result: **mean 4.44, median 4** (AR: 4.81 / 5). Four 5s, five 4s.

### 2.2 The one dash exception — Wisp's Blink at 4, not 5

Wisp is the only character who is simultaneously the roster's **lowest HP (85)** and holds a
**range-2 basic** (Dagger Flurry). Blink is both her approach *and* her exit, and it carries nothing
— at 5 she cannot open a fight and survive it inside one cooldown cycle, which deletes the archetype
rather than taxing it. AR kept exactly this exception: PuP, the low-HP harasser, is one of only two
lancers in the game with a sub-4 dash (Pounce, 2). We stay inside the owner's band by pricing Blink
at **4**, the floor, rather than going under it. **Flagged for playtest** — if Wisp still reads as
untouchable after DEATH-HANG lands and a real two-machine game happens, this is the first number to
revisit.

### 2.3 Non-basic Blast — 3 is the number, 4 is earned

> **A non-basic blast costs 3. It costs 4 only if its damage exceeds the roster's undelayed skill
> ceiling (24, `roster-v1.md` §4).**

| Character | Blast | Payload | Now | **New** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aegis | Grounding Strike | damage 14 + slow | 2 | **3** |
| Cinder | Flare Burst | damage 10 + DoT 6/2 + reveal 2 | 2 | **3** |
| Kestrel | Kite Shot | damage 16 + slow | 2 | **3** |
| Lumen | Dazzling Ray | damage 12 + weaken | 2 | **3** |
| Ravok | Shockwave | damage 12 + slow | 2 | **3** |
| Wisp | Bola | damage 12 + slow | 2 | **3** |
| Bastion | Chain Hook | damage 10 + pull 2 | 3 | **3** (unchanged — already in band) |
| Vex | Frag Grenade | damage 34 | 3 | **4** |

Result: **mean 3.13, median 3** (AR: 3.55 / 4).

**No blast is priced below 3, and that is the judgement call.** Every non-basic blast in CARDS
already carries a status rider — slow, weaken, reveal, DoT, or a pull — on top of its damage. None of
them is a plain shot, and a rider every other turn is precisely the thing that made the free basic
feel like filler. **The plain shot is the 0-cooldown basic**, and restoring that hierarchy is the
whole point of the directive.

**Vex's Frag Grenade to 4** is the single upward exception: at 34 damage it *is* the roster's skill
nuke ceiling (`roster-v1.md` §4 names it), it is the only skill that beats the undelayed cap of 24,
and AR priced its equivalents higher still (Nix Vortex Round 5, Gremolitions Big Bang 5, Zuki Rocket
Jump 6).

**Bastion's Chain Hook stays at 3** — it is already inside the band, so the directive does not compel
a change. It is nonetheless the roster's only pull ≥ 2 (`roster-v1.md` §4 displacement budget) and
therefore the **watch-item** if the blast band needs a second 4 after playtest.

## 3. What this does to the game — three checks the Builder does *not* need to re-derive

### 3.1 The ultimate clock does not slow down. It speeds up slightly.

The obvious worry — longer cooldowns mean fewer casts, fewer casts mean less energy, ults arrive late
— **is wrong here, and the reason is structural.** GAME_SPEC §3 fixes **one ability per turn** (the
4-move budget). Raising a cooldown therefore never reduces the number of casts per turn; it only
changes *which* ability is cast. And in this roster **dashes are the cheapest energy abilities we
have** (`energyGain` 4–5: Backdraft 4, Combat Roll 4, Blink 4, Intercept 5, Skim 5, Glimmer Step 5,
Bramble Stride 5) while **every basic pays 8**. Pushing dashes out replaces a 4–5 turn with an 8.

- **Net positive** for Aegis, Cinder, Kestrel, Lumen, Thorn, Vex, Wisp.
- **Net neutral** for Bastion and Ravok (Ram Charge and Bullrush both already pay 8).
- The blast band is energy-neutral (every non-basic blast pays 8, same as the basic); the only debit
  anywhere is Vex's Frag Grenade at 10 → −2 on the turns he no longer throws it, comfortably covered
  by Combat Roll's +4.

**Conclusion: `roster-v1.md` §4's "ultimates come online turns 8–10" survives this change unmodified.
Do not retune `energyGain` to compensate — there is nothing to compensate for.** This is called out
explicitly so a Builder does not add a "helpful" energy pass on top.

### 3.2 Usage counts, in the format that actually ships

2v2 is the default and its turn limit is **16** (`formats.ts`, GAME_SPEC §1). The engine sets
`cooldowns[id] = cooldown` on use and ticks −1 at end of turn (`resolve.ts:903`, `:2665`), so an
ability cast on turn *T* returns on turn *T + cd*.

| cd | Casts in a 16-turn 2v2 | Turns |
|---|---|---|
| 2 (today) | 8 | 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15 |
| 3 | 6 | 1, 4, 7, 10, 13, 16 |
| 4 | 4 | 1, 5, 9, 13 |
| 5 | 4 | 1, 6, 11, 16 |

So a dash goes from **eight** uses a match to **four**, and a non-basic blast from eight to six.
Turn-1 openings are unchanged (everything starts off cooldown); the change is felt from turn 2.

### 3.3 The free-action tax is untouched

The `free: true` cooldown tax (`edge-cases.md` "A free action grants no energy and pays for itself in
cooldown"; `free-actions-and-catalysts.md` §1.2) applies to **Vex Overwatch Trap 4, Thorn Snare Bloom
3, Wisp Veil & Decoy 5, Cinder Stoke the Flame 4** — all **Prep**, all covered by directive #3, all
unchanged. **No changed ability is `free: true`.** For the record: if a dash or blast is ever
converted to a free action, the +1 tax stacks on the *new* band (a disengage dash would land at 6).

## 4. The rule that let this drift, and how it gets closed

`docs/design/roster-v1.md` §1 states the kit constraint as **"Skills | 3 | `cooldown ≥ 2`"**. That
floor is the reason the roster clustered at 2: nothing in the repo ever said a dash should cost more
than a heal. The new numbers do not violate it (5 ≥ 2), so **the prose does not block the data
change** — but a bare floor cannot stop the next character from shipping at 2 again.

Two follow-ups, in different hands:

- **Enforcement (Builder, this batch):** a per-phase band invariant in
  `packages/engine/test/content.test.ts`, which already imports all nine characters and is where the
  roster's structural rules are asserted. See **CD-BAND-INVARIANT** in BACKLOG. The invariant needs
  **no allow-list** — with Blink at 4 rather than 3, every value in the roster is inside its band.
- **Prose (Designer/owner):** `roster-v1.md` §1 should carry the band rule itself, and §2/§5 the
  commitment gradient behind it. Design docs are Designer-owned — routed as a flag, not an item.

wait an ability off cooldown will need its turn count raised. Run the full suite; do not adjust a
data value to keep a test green.
