## Done well (one line)

CHASE1 landed the load-bearing ruling as a *consequence of structure, not a special case* — the
"stop at the last-known square" behaviour falls out of `pathToward` choosing the closest reachable
square, and the fog test asserts on the chaser's final **position**, so any implementation that
peeked at `target.pos` fails it. That is the right way to make a hidden-info rule unbreakable.

## Issues, ranked

No correctness or determinism defects in the batch. Everything below is a scoping gap or a ruling
to ratify — nothing to fix in shipped code.

### 1. [MED — playtest blocker] Power-up pads are invisible in the client (Builder OQ #4)
PADS1 was scoped ENGINE + DATA, so both maps now carry pads no player can see — a pickup shows only
in the combat log. Pads that read as random buffs will poison any pad playtest. **Fix: backlog
PADS-INDICATOR** — a ground marker per pad, the analogue of TRAP-INDICATOR (colour by type,
show-when-armed vs consumed). Needed before the maps are playtested.

### 2. [LOW — Designer, not a build item] Pad placement/timings are unreviewed (Builder OQ #3)
The shipped pads are **Builder placeholders** (three mirrored pairs per map, `firstTurn: 2,
everyTurns: 4`) — plausible, not designed; the centre-line pairs make map-centre contested in a way
nobody has playtested. The MAP-CAPS-style mirror guard keeps retuning safe. **Route to Designer**
(pad squares/timings are `data/`); flag for playtest. Not a Builder item.

### 3. [LOW — coverage gap, carried] No e2e for chase route, ghosts, or the camo red tile
All three are new render styles unit-covered but not pixel-verified; RENDER-VERIFY has caught two
bugs unit tests structurally could not. Each needs a multi-turn drive (a ghost needs an enemy to
enter then leave vision; a chase needs a target that moves). **Fix: backlog RENDER-COVERAGE** — one
multi-turn RENDER-VERIFY drive exercising a chase, a ghost, a red tile, and (once built) a pad
marker. Cheap to add alongside PADS-INDICATOR; not blocking.
