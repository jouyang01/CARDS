## Issues, ranked

### 1. [Re-scope, not a defect] MET1 invalidates shipped MV3/MV4; A1's SVG is superseded by RND1
The owner's new batch changes the distance metric (Chebyshev→Manhattan, MET1) and the
renderer (SVG→orthographic 3D, RND1). Handled explicitly this session: the MV3 diagonal
**cost model** and MV4 are marked **superseded** in edge-cases (every diagonal now costs 2,
not the 1/2 alternation) — not deleted, with a pointer to MET1; A1's keyed-SVG impl is
superseded while its **principle** (persistent scene objects keyed by `unitId`) carries into
the RND1 re-spec. No shipped code is wrong *today*; it's scheduled for replacement, not left
to rot. Scheduled below in the revised sequencing.

### 2. [Low — mooted by RND1] HP bars scale with zoom (A3 Open Question)
The "bars outside `g.world`" vs "WAAPI camera, no rAF" conflict the Builder flagged **goes
away under RND1** — a 3D renderer billboards UI independently of world zoom. Re-address in the
RND1/A1-respec (billboarded HP bars/labels), not in the SVG path being deleted.

### 3. [Low — deferred] `heal`/`statusApplied` lack a source
The same additive A0 change would let shield/heal flourishes animate. Not needed until the FX
layer exists (**A4**, deferred) — bundle it there rather than adding unused fields now.

No correctness defects in the shipped A0/A1/A2/A3/M2/D1-dash.
