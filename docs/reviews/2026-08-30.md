## Issues, ranked

No correctness defects in the shipped batch. The items below are the **owner's new rulings** routed
to backlog, plus the one bug STEALTH-CONFIRM surfaced.

### 1. [HIGH — one-line data fix, owner-ruled] Wisp's Stealth is unobservable (`duration: 1`)
Applied in Prep, ticked off at that turn's end-of-turn tick (GAME_SPEC §6) — gone before the enemy's
next Decision, while the decoy stands through `castTurn + 1`. The enemy is shown a Wisp *and* a
decoy in one square, reading as "stealth is broken" (Dev Note #4, all four prior sessions). **The
owner's Dev Note Ruling — "Stealth/Decoy should last one turn AFTER the skill is used" — fixes it:
`data/characters/wisp.json` `veil_and_decoy` Stealth `duration: 1 → 2`** (covers cast turn + next,
matching the decoy). **Fix: backlog STEALTH-DURATION.** Ruled in edge-cases; resolves Builder OQ #1.

### 2. [MED — engine + client] Camouflage reveal fires on too few triggers (owner vision rule)
The owner's Rule 2: acting **while in a camouflage tile / Stealth** — offensive ability, **catalyst**,
or **taking damage** — reveals you this turn + next and turns the tile red. The engine applies
`reveal` **only on dealing damage** (`resolve.ts:911, 1101`); **taking damage** only calls
`breakStealth` (`:1079`) — so a *brush*-hidden unit that is hit keeps its concealment next turn — and
**catalyst use** reveals nothing. **Fix: backlog CAMO-REVEAL** — expand the triggers and gate them
on *being concealed at the moment of acting* (so acting in the open arms no future reveal), plus the
client "tile turns red" tell. Ruled in edge-cases.

### 3. [MED — client, newly scoped] No last-known-position ghosts (owner vision rule)
Rule 1: an enemy you've lost sight of should stay drawn as a **ghost at its last-spotted square**
until re-spotted; attacking from positional fog shows only the trajectory, not your model. The
engine already keeps the model hidden (`canSee` tests range/LoS *before* concealment, so `reveal`
never overrides fog) — the missing half is the client ghost memory. Was "out of scope" until now;
the owner asked for it. **Fix: backlog LAST-KNOWN.** Ruled in edge-cases.

### 4. [LOW-MED — engine tests + client] Dash-onto-occupied prohibition + knockback exception
Owner: "you should not be able to dash onto the same square as another character unless there's a
knockback." **The prohibition already holds** — teleport fizzles on an occupant (`resolve.ts:292`),
a charge rests on the furthest free square (`walkCharge:933`). Owed: (a) regression tests pinning it
across teleport/charge/Shift; (b) the **knockback exception** — a dash whose own knockback clears
the destination resolves that displacement *in Dash, before landing* (no current skill exercises it
— forward-looking); (c) client refuses committing a teleport aim onto an occupied square (silent
fizzle otherwise). **Fix: backlog DASH-OCCUPIED.** Ruled in edge-cases.
