## Issues, ranked — the chase bug leads

### 1. [HIGH — engine correctness, owner-directed, 4th report] The chase is capped by vision RANGE, not line of sight (Dev Note #2)

*"Chasing is still not working. If a character is one tile away, it will only chase 1 tile even if
the target sprints 8 tiles away … get the chasing character AS CLOSE to the target as possible based
on remaining movement and assuming they have vision … If they lose vision … as close to the last
place the chase target was seen."* **CHASE-FOLLOW shipped and does not fix this.** It re-checks sight
each step, but the check is `teamCanSee`, whose first gate is `distance > VISION_RANGE → false`
(`vision.ts:253`). A target that outruns **range 6** is treated as fogged **even while the chaser is
directly behind it in the open**, and the per-step re-check cannot rescue it — from every square the
chaser may reach, the target is still beyond 6.

Reproduced against the engine: chaser one tile behind a target that sprints 8 east moves **exactly
one tile** and stops, `seen:false` — the owner's report verbatim. **Root cause: the engine conflates
"line of sight" (what the owner writes) with "vision range" (the 6-tile radius).** `canSee` composes
three independent gates — range (`distance ≤ VISION_RANGE`), line of sight (`hasLineOfSight`, walls),
concealment (`isConcealedFrom`, brush/stealth/reveal).

**Fix: backlog CHASE-LOS (engine, HIGH).** The chase's visibility predicate keeps line-of-sight and
concealment but **drops the range gate** — a chase sees its quarry whenever an unobstructed,
unconcealed sightline exists, at any distance; it follows to the true square, closing by its full
movement. "Lose vision" then means what the owner means: a **wall** breaks the sightline or the
target enters **brush/Stealth** — only then does the chase fall to the last-known square and stop.
Golden rule #5 holds where it bites (a terrain/brush/stealth-hidden target is never pursued to its
true square); the only widening is pursuit along a clear open sightline past 6 tiles, which is the
chase the owner is asking for. Everything else in CHASE-FOLLOW (per-step re-derivation, last-known
fallback, determinism) stands; only the predicate changes, and **last-known recording keeps the
range cap.** Ruled in edge-cases (CHASE-LOS). The reported case flips to end **adjacent / `seen:true`**.

### 2. [MED — engine, owner-directed] A blocked/contested blink should land adjacent, not nowhere (Dev Note #3)

The owner answers OQ #1 as a directive: *"Blocked blink should land adjacent instead of not at all."*
The shipped BLINK-CLASH resolves two-blinks-one-square as "neither lands"; the owner wants both to
land, on the nearest legal square. **Fix: backlog BLINK-ADJ (engine)** — a blink whose destination
is blocked (wall), occupied (a resting unit), or contested (another blink) lands on the nearest legal
square to the destination (Manhattan-nearest, `direction8`/row-major tie-break); contested blinkers
take distinct nearest squares in unit order. Supersedes "neither lands." Ruled in edge-cases (BLINK-ADJ).
