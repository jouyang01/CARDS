## Issues, ranked — the Dev Notes, four of them real engine/spec bugs

### 1. [HIGH — combat correctness] Cone attacks pass through walls (Dev Note #7)
*"my conal/straight line attacks are going past the gray blocks."* **Root cause:** `coneSquares`
(`shapes.ts:255,270`) **drops the wall tiles but does not occlude the squares behind them** — a
cone reaches straight through a wall. `lineSquares` stops at the first wall on its axis (`:208`) but
its HITBOX1 half-tile side-band can still cover a tile behind a wall. **Fix: backlog LOS-OCCLUSION**
— filter every `line`/`cone` covered tile by **`hasLineOfSight(caster, tile)`** (the engine's own
LoS kernel — walls block, cover does not), so a wall shadows the whole wedge, not just the axis;
`circle`/`square` stay un-occluded (lobbed). Determinism-safe (reuses an integer kernel; no trig).
Ruled in edge-cases. **The most important fix in the batch — attacks hitting through walls is a
fairness bug.**

### 2. [MED — combat] Melee attacks don't ignore cover (Dev Note #8)
*"Melee attacks should ignore COVER, but not the full vision."* `isBehindCover` exempts `range ≤ 1`
(`combat.ts:175`) — but under Manhattan a melee ability that reaches a diagonal neighbour is
authored at **range 2**, so the heuristic misfires and point-blank melee still eats cover. **Fix:
backlog MELEE-COVER** — key melee-ignores-cover off an explicit **`melee: true` ability flag**
(Designer-authored), not a range threshold; the engine skips the cover reduction for it, LoS/walls
still apply. Ruled in edge-cases; the "which abilities are melee" data pass routes to the Designer.

### 3. [MED — hidden-info leak] The move preview reveals invisible enemies (Dev Note #1)
*"Move command is blocked if an enemy is out of line of sight but on the tile that you are trying to
move. This is giving unintentional information."* The client's `reachableSquares`/`pathTo` treat
**every** unit as an obstacle, fogged enemies included, so a path that reroutes around an invisible
enemy leaks its position. **Fix: backlog MOVE-FOG** — plan-time reachability uses the **team-visible
unit set only**; the engine (true board) stops the mover short at resolution, and *that* reveals the
enemy. Same golden-rule-#5 principle as PREVIEW-FOG. Client-only. Ruled in edge-cases.

### 4. [MED — mechanic] A chase can't sprint (Dev Note #4)
*"Chase should be able to sprint or move depending on how many actions the character has … full
sprinting chase"* when no attack (or only a free action) was used. **Fix: backlog CHASE-SPRINT** —
a chase's budget follows the sprint rule: **8 when no normal ability is declared, 4 when one is** (a
free action doesn't block it). Ruled in edge-cases.

### 5. [LOW — e2e red, pre-existing] Two RENDER-COVERAGE drives fail (Builder OQ #10)
`arming a chase draws a route` and `an enemy is drawn on a fogged board (LAST-KNOWN)` both fail with
"never got an enemy into sight" — **on `main` too** (verified at `c6a64ba`). The `walkToCentre`
helper advances ~1 square/turn routing around `duel-arena`'s wall between spawn and centre, so 5
turns never close the 13-square gap; likely the **PADS-SPREAD pad re-placement (PR #51)** made a
marginal drive fail. **Fix: backlog RENDER-DRIVE-FIX** — re-spec the helper (drive with `Sprint`,
or aim at a wall-free row, or raise the turn cap). The suite is red until this lands.
