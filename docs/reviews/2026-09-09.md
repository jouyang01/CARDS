## The Dev Notes

### 1. [HIGH — engine correctness, owner-directed, repeated] Chase freezes vision at the origin (Dev Note #1)

*"Chasing still isn't working as intended. You should follow the character that you're chasing all
the way until you lose line of sight or you run out of movement."* Root-caused. `planChases`
(`resolve.ts:1552-1556`) measures `teamCanSee` **once, with the chaser still on its starting
square**, and `pathToward` then walks toward that single frozen goal. So a target that outran the
*stationary* chaser's vision by even one square is treated as fully fogged, and the chase halts at
the last-known square **even when arriving there restores sight and budget remains**.

Reproduced against the engine: `a`(5,10) chasing `e` that ran to (12,10) stops at **(9,10)** —
three short, `seen:false` — because (12,10) is 7 from a's origin and vision is 6; yet from (9,10)
the target sits 3 away in plain view. Sprint budget (8) is untouched. The CHASE1 case *"follows a
target that ran"* asserts this (9,10) while its own comment says the chaser "ends up adjacent" — the
assertion was fitted to the bug. Prior chase work (CHASE1, CHASE-SPRINT) fixed the budget and the
fog-honesty but never the origin-frozen vision, which is why the report keeps coming back.

**Fix: backlog CHASE-FOLLOW (engine, HIGH).** Resolve the chase as an iterative walk on the frozen
post-Move snapshot, re-deriving the goal from the chaser's **live** square each step: while the team
can see the target, step toward its true square; while it cannot, step toward last-known. Stop only
on caught/arrived, on genuinely-lost sight (standing on last-known, target still unseen), or on
exhausted movement. **Golden rule #5 holds** — every step aims at a square the chaser can see from
where it stands, or at its own memory; nothing leaks. Ruled in edge-cases (CHASE-FOLLOW). The
open-map *"follows a target that ran"* case flips to **adjacent / `seen:true`**; the brush fog cases
stay green.
