## Issues, ranked — the eight Dev Notes, root-caused

All eight are **client-side**; the engine enforces the rules they ask to be *shown*.

### 1. [HIGH — orders silently dropped] Range is not shown or enforced on the client (Dev Notes #1, #2, #8)
*"Veil's Blink looks like you can use it where you want, it should be limited to the range … Aegis's
intercept does the same"* (#8); *"Dash catalyst doesn't have a range indicator"* (#1); *"Overwatch
Trap doesn't have a range indicator either … audit … all skills"* (#2). **The engine enforces
range** — `aimIsLegal` → `aimInRange` for `square`/`circle` (`resolve.ts:410`), a cost-bounded path
for `path` — and **drops** an out-of-range order. The client has two gaps: **(a)** the range
envelope is drawn only for the hovered/selected *normal* ability (`app.ts:497
envelopeAbility = hovered ?? chosen`), so an armed **free ability** (#2) and **catalyst** (#1) show
none; **(b)** `aimFor` for `square`/`circle` returns the **raw clicked square** (`targeting.ts:413`)
and `onBoardClick` commits it with **no `aimLegal` gate** (`app.ts:795-816`) — so Blink and
Intercept (both `square` teleports) accept any click and the engine then silently drops the order.
Only `path` dashes are gated (`pathToExact` → empty). **Fix: backlog AIM-RANGE** — envelope for
every armed aimable slot + reject/clamp out-of-range commits, audited over all shapes × all slots.
Ruled in edge-cases.

### 2. [MED-HIGH — hidden-info leak] Preview numbers reveal unseen enemies (Dev Note #6)
*"The preview of damage/healing cannot show up if the player taking the action does not have vision
of the character affected."* `preview-numbers.ts:76` filters affected units by polarity/team but
**not by vision** — a red number floats over a fogged enemy, leaking its exact square. **Fix:
backlog PREVIEW-FOG** — show a per-unit preview only when the acting seat's team can see that unit
(`visibleEnemiesForTeam`, the same gate fog uses). Free-aim into fog still stands; you just aren't
told what's there. Ruled in edge-cases.

### 3. [MED — feature missing] Traps have no board indicator (Dev Note #5)
*"Traps need an indicator on the ground for the team or teams who can see it."* Placed traps are in
`state.traps` (with `owner` + `pos`) but the client draws **nothing** — only a combat-log line on
trigger. A player can't see their own minefield. **Fix: backlog TRAP-INDICATOR** — draw traps on
the ground: the **placing team always** sees its own; the **enemy** sees one only with team vision
of the square. Pure consumer (reads `state.traps` + `visibleSquaresForTeam`). Ruled in edge-cases.

### 4. [MED — indicator wrong] Shift doesn't render as a yellow dash route (Dev Note #7)
*"Shift's dash catalyst should show as a yellow movement similar to other dash/blinks."* `dashRoute`
(the yellow route line) is only called for the *normal* ability (`app.ts:593 dashRoute(unit, chosen,
…)`); a Dash catalyst's aim is drawn as a catalyst-coloured area overlay, not a yellow route. **Fix:
backlog DASH-CAT-ROUTE** — a dash-phase catalyst (Shift) draws the same yellow route + destination
marker a blink does.

### 5. [LOW-MED — HUD label] Catalysts don't show their cost (Dev Note #3)
*"Prep Catalyst and Blast Catalyst are not showing as free actions."* `HudCatalyst` (`hud.ts:44`)
carries `phase` but no cost field — free *abilities* show a "free" tag (`hud.ts:269`), catalysts do
not. Post-CAT-DASH-COST the labels differ by colour: **Prep/Blast = "free", Dash = "costs Move".**
**Fix: backlog CAT-COST-LABEL** — add the cost tag to catalyst slots.

### 6. [LOW — verify] Veil's stealth (Dev Note #4)
*"Does Veil's Stealth work? It doesn't seem to be working."* STATUS-AUDIT proves it **engine-side**
(a stealthed unit leaves `visibleEnemiesForTeam`), and this batch also shipped **FREE-UI** (you can
now cast the free Veil & Decoy *and* take your turn) and **DECOY-RENDER** (the decoy is now
team-aware and fogged, so it no longer plants a Wisp-marker on the hidden square). The most likely
explanation is a pre-batch build — but it is worth a **STEALTH-CONFIRM**: an e2e that a stealthed
Wisp is absent from the enemy seat's render and its decoy shows as an enemy Wisp. Low cost, high
certainty.

No correctness defects in the shipped engine work; the one bug the batch *introduced-adjacent*
(range not gated client-side) predates it — the engine was always the enforcer.
