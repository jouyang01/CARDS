## Verification

- `npm test` → **3219 passing** (1882 + 1035 + 302). `npm run typecheck` clean. Engine purity grep clean.
- **COVER-EDGE — the one engine change, and it is sound.** A cover entry is now the old full-square block
  **or** a directional barricade `{x,y,facing}`: walk onto the tile to take cover, cannot cross the faced
  edge (both ways), occupant reduced 50% against attacks whose line crosses that edge, **does not block
  LoS**, melee ignores it — owner-chosen AR half-wall semantics. Verified:
  - **Determinism/purity intact.** The new `geometry.ts` (`segmentsIntersect`, `edgeCorners`, `FACING_VEC`)
    is **integer cross-products** — `(a.x-o.x)*(b.y-o.y) − (a.y-o.y)*(b.x-o.x)` — no RNG, no trig, no
    division, no game-value floats; edge corners are exact half-integers. Purity grep clean.
  - **One geometry shared by movement and combat** — "a barricade you cannot shoot a defender across is one
    you cannot walk across either, by construction." `coverEdgeBlocks` gates both `reachableSquares` and
    `validateMovePath`; `blocksMovement` now stops only walls + full-block; `isBehindCover` gained the
    occupant-edge case. Coexists with full-block cover, so **iron-basin is untouched** (opt-in via `facing`).
  - **Tested** (`cover-edge.test.ts`, `melee-cover.test.ts`, the `content.test.ts` fairness guard). Phase
    order (Prep→Dash→Blast→Move) and dash immunity are not touched — cover is a combat/movement modifier.
    N-safe (per-tile predicate, per-unit checks). Discriminated `CoverEdge`/`isCoverEdge` types are clean.
- **Proving Grounds** — duel-arena rebuilt at 17×11, 2 spawns/side, 1v1/2v2 only (iron-basin owns 4v4),
  **180° rotational symmetry** (the fairness invariant moved from left-right mirror to rotational; both maps
  pass; pads stay on the mirror check). Sound, and in the Designer/map lane — though note it **reached into
  `packages/engine`** for COVER-EDGE (a genuine engine mechanic); shipped clean and tested, so acknowledged
  rather than objected to, but flagging that the map lane crossed the role boundary.
- **Board polish + VFX** — chamfers found two of their own bugs by test (inward winding, undersized inset),
  jitter is **hashed from tile coords not a counter** (so draw-order can't desync it — correct instinct),
  debris keyed on *that something got hit*, not who hit it. All display-only.

## Done well (one line)

COVER-EDGE unified movement and combat on **one** geometry predicate rather than two that could drift —
"cannot shoot across ⇒ cannot walk across, by construction" is exactly the kind of invariant-by-design that
stops a whole class of future desync bugs.

## Dev Note routed — the friend-or-foe colour system

*"We want to solve for the issue of mirror matchups and identifying if it's friend or foe … similar to
Atlas Reactor …"* This is real and confirmed in the code: the client colours by **absolute team**
(`team0 = 0x4f8cff blue`, `team1 = 0xff6b5e red`, `renderer3d.ts:1645`), so **a player on team 1 sees
themselves red and the enemy blue** — and there is already a `renderer3d.ts:2306` comment conceding a colour
is *"only right when the viewer is team 0."* Ruled **FOF-COLORS** in edge-cases (viewer-relative friend/foe:
**self blue / ally green / foe red** on units; **friendly blue / foe red** on committed overlays). Split
into two backlog items:
- **FOF-UNITS** (the mirror solver, priority) — viewer-relative unit tint/outline + a **foot ring** + the
  nameplate. Fixes the team-1-viewer inversion directly.
- **FOF-OVERLAYS** — committed ability lines, move arrows and Blast/AoE templates in friendly-blue / foe-red
  so an ally's ult and an enemy mirror's are never confused (Dev Note part 2). The viewer's own in-progress
  aim keeps the meaning-coded palette; reconciling FoF overlay colour with the meaning-coded vocabulary
  (AOE-CLASH / OVERLAY-BY-THEME) is a **Designer call**, flagged.

Both routed verbatim in BACKLOG.
