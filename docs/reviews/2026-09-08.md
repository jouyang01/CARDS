## Issues, ranked — the Dev Notes, one a real engine bug

### 1. [HIGH — engine correctness, owner-directed] Reveal-on-attack is unconditional (Dev Note #4)
*"Why are characters being debuffed with 'revealed' when they hit an enemy. This is incorrect …
when a character attacks from an area where enemies lack line of sight or vision, attack and
movement remain completely hidden."* The engine applies `reveal` **unconditionally when a hit
lands** (`resolve.ts:1138-1141` + the dash `hitEnemy` branch) — so an attacker on open ground or
behind a wall still picks up a `reveal` debuff (a no-op mechanically, but NAMEPLATE-LAYOUT now
*shows* it, reading as punishment for attacking). **Fix: backlog REVEAL-FIX** — route the
reveal-on-hit through the **same `revealIfConcealed` gate as CAMO-REVEAL**: a unit is revealed by
attacking **iff it was concealed by brush/Stealth**. Open attacker → no reveal; positional-fog
attacker → no reveal and stays fully hidden (the owner's rule); brush/Stealth attacker → revealed
(the camo tell, unchanged). **Reverses the 2026-08-31 "reveal whether hidden or not" correction**
(the owner has directed the opposite). Ruled in edge-cases; the `attribution.test.ts` open-attacker
case flips.

### 2. [IMPORTANT — engine] Adopt AR's clash rules (Dev Note #2, + subsumes MOVE-SPRINT-FIRST)
The owner supplied AR's clash rules verbatim; the shipped `stepMovers` stops **all** same-step
co-targets, where AR stops **only an ender** — a passer-through continues. **Fix: backlog CLASH-AR**
(engine, IMPORTANT) — passers continue; two pad amendments (same-step entry claims nothing; an ender
outranks a passer); 2-cycle swap block unchanged; per-phase. The owner's theory that the "first
sprint doesn't move" report is a clash artefact (a sprint path halted whole by the stops-all rule)
is plausible and this is the fix — **re-verify MOVE-SPRINT-FIRST after CLASH-AR.** Ruled in
edge-cases (supersedes the PROPOSED CL1).

### 3. [MED — client] Move-onto-occupied doesn't move; body-clicks select the wrong tile (Dev Note #3)
*"When moving to a location that another character occupies, the character should move to the
closest possible location. Right now, the character does not move at all."* Root cause (Designer OQ
#2, ruled): `squareFromPoint` raycasts the **ground plane only**, so a click on a unit's body (a box
lifted ~0.6) resolves to the tile *behind* it — the move goes to the wrong tile or nowhere. **Fix:
backlog BODY-CLICK** — raycast unit meshes first, prefer a visible-unit hit; MOVE1's nearest-legal
routing then applies to the correct (occupied) tile so the mover steps adjacent. Verify MOVE1's
`pathTo` still routes to nearest-legal for a directly-clicked occupied tile (no separate regression).
Ruled by the Designer (§4.3).

### 4. [MED — pad visibility, fixed in data; guard owed] Shadow-row pad moves (Builder OQ #1)
PADS-PLACEMENT had put Health/Might pads one row north of a wall, in the camera's occlusion shadow →
invisible. The Designer moved them off the shadow rows (both maps, mirror-verified) and ruled the
**shadow-row rule** (no pad on a square whose y+1 neighbour is wall/cover). **Owed: backlog
SHADOW-ROW-TEST** — a `content.test.ts` guard next to PADS-SPREAD (Builder). The renderer lever was
correctly rejected (a pad drawn over a wall lies about occlusion).

### 5. [LOW — uplift] The unique-basics engine knobs (Designer §3)
