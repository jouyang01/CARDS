## Issues, ranked

### 1. [Medium — Dev Note gap] Client can't do "move AND shoot" in one turn
The engine and `targeting.toUnitOrders` already support ability + a 4-square move together
(and 8-square sprint when move-only). But the UI flow makes them **mutually exclusive**:
`app.ts` `selectAbility` sets `draft.movePath = []` and `selectMove` sets
`draft.abilityId = undefined`, and the move preview only shows when no ability is chosen
(`app.ts:118`). So a player cannot pick an ability *and* draw a move. **Fix: backlog MS1**
(client) — let ability and move coexist (ability → aim → also draw a ≤budget move; sprint
and dash stay exclusive with an ability). Engine needs no change. This is the human's Dev
Note this session.

### 2. [Low — interim, bundled Designer ask] amount-1 charge knockback nets zero
`resolve.applyDisplacements`: a charge that overshoots and whose knockback lands the victim
exactly on the charger's square yields no net displacement (co-occupancy invariant wins;
Ram Charge). Ruled acceptable interim (edge-cases); the real fix (carry-through vs swap) is
bundled into the Designer charge-combat ENGINE ASK. Not blocking.

### 3. [Low — flag] Charge paths are still orthogonal while Move is 8-direction
`aimIsLegal` `path` case rejects diagonal charge steps, so a player who can move diagonally
can't charge diagonally. Inconsistent but harmless. **Fix: backlog MV4** (diagonal charge
paths — independent of the charge-*damage* ruling; just extend the path check + corner-cut).
Low priority; confirm intent.

No correctness defects in the shipped MV1-fix / MV3.
