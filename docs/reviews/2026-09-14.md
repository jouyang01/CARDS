## Issues, ranked

No defects this session. The five items below are Open-Question rulings, not bugs.

### 1. [INFO — ruled] Two small networked-loop gaps become items (OQ #2, #4)

- **WAYPOINT-DASH-CLEAR (OQ #2):** a composed move route correctly survives a *non-dash* ability, but
  a **dash IS the movement** (the engine already drops the `movePath`), so a committed dash must also
  clear the route and its marks on screen — else the board draws a path that won't execute. Ruled;
  small client item.
- **M3-END-SCREEN (OQ #4):** the loop now closes (create → pick → play → resolve) but a decided match
  leaves the player on the final board with no winner and no way out. Ruled; client item (applies to
  the hot-seat game too).

### 2. [INFO — ruled] M3-TIMER placement + beam/axis composition (OQ #3, #5)

- **M3-TIMER (OQ #3):** the countdown sits **beside** the wait banner in the `UI-TIMER` slot, not over
  it — the banner says *what* you wait for, the clock says *how long*; both are needed. Folded into
  M3-TIMER's spec. On timeout: **missed submission → hold position** (unchanged).
- **Beam + axisBonus (OQ #5):** they **compose** and are not forbidden — a beam's axis is its centre
  file, exactly where `axisBonus` adds. No validator line owed; recorded so the legality isn't
  "fixed" away.
