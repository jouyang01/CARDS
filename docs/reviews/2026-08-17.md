## Movement vs the AR wiki (now that I have the text)

**Matches AR (verified → promoted to RULED in edge-cases):** 4/8 ranges; Haste/Slow (6/12,
2/4) and cancel; Root = 0; Unstoppable immune to slow/root/displacement; ground vs airborne
dash = walked charge vs teleport (traps + walls behavior); Rooted doesn't stop a dash;
collisions ("can't end on an occupied square; closest holds it; applies even if allied");
displacement resolves in Blast and cancels Move.

**Diverges from AR:**
1. **Diagonal movement — NOT implemented (headline).** AR is 8-direction with "every second
   diagonal costs 2 squares" (~1.5 each; 9-instead-of-8 with one diagonal). The engine is
   strictly orthogonal. **Human decision this session: adopt full AR diagonal movement** →
   backlog **MV3** (and GAME_SPEC §3 must change). Large: touches `board.ts` (8-dir),
   `movement.ts` (parity-aware weighted pathing), and all movement tests.
2. **Clashes are stricter than AR.** AR lets two units *both pass through* the same square
   (neither ending) and both continue; our `stepMovers` stops all same-step co-targets.
   Documented as a v1 simplification → optional **CL1**.
3. **Multiple simultaneous displacements** apply sequentially (collection order) rather than
   AR's vector-sum. Rare in v1 → optional **CL2**.
4. **Following** (move to a teammate's adjacent square) — not implemented; 2v2 UX, future.

## Issues, ranked

### 1. [Medium — regression from MV1] Ram Charge no longer displaces its target
`resolve.walkCharge`/`runDash`: a charge now passes through and lands *beyond* its target, and
knockback is measured from origin — so the charger's own landing square can block the victim's
push (the Builder flagged: "Vex is struck but not displaced"). **Fix (edge-cases ruling +
backlog MV1-fix):** displacement resolution must ignore the *displacing attacker's own body*
as an obstacle. Small, mechanical. The separate targeting question (first/all/destination)
stays a Designer ENGINE ASK.

### 2. [Large — approved rework] Adopt diagonal movement (MV3)
Not a defect — the approved AR-fidelity change. Sized as its own item; see backlog MV3 for the
cost model, corner-cut default (confirm), and the GAME_SPEC §3 update. Do MV1-fix and MV3 as
the movement batch; keep CL1/CL2 optional.

### 3. [Low] `stepMovers` clash strictness (CL1) and sequential displacement (CL2)
Below AR fidelity but deterministic and rarely visible at 2–4 units. Optional; ruled/PROPOSED
in edge-cases. Only pursue if the human wants exact AR clashes.

No correctness defects beyond #1.
