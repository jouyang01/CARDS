## Issues, ranked

### 1. [Medium — FF1 incomplete] Friendly fire not applied to charges or delayed detonations
FF1 landed for direct Blast but two harmful-damage paths still filter to enemies:
- `resolve.walkCharge` (~:588) strikes the first **enemy** crossed — under FF1 it should strike
  the first **unit** crossed (ally or enemy); `chargeHits:"all"` → all units. (Builder's
  Open Question — they correctly didn't assume.)
- `resolve.detonateDelayedBlasts` (~:768) skips own-team units — a grenade should hit allies in
  its area too.
Both are inconsistencies with the FF1 ruling. **Fix: backlog FF1-charge + FF1-delayed** (ruled
in edge-cases; energy stays enemy-only; supersedes the "first enemy" wording of R1a/R1b).

### 2. [Low — consistency] Teleport-strike adjacency still Chebyshev
`resolve.runDash` — Shadowstep hits the 8 Chebyshev neighbours while everything else is
Manhattan. **Ruled to Manhattan-≤1 (4 orthogonal)** for consistency (backlog **MET1-tp**);
flags a Wisp rebalance for the Designer (Shadowstep now catches 4 squares, not 8).

### 3. [Low — likely client gap] "Dash into bushes" — engine already allows it
`teleport`/`blocksMovement` both permit brush (only wall/cover/oob block), so dashing/moving
into brush works in the engine. The owner's note implies the **client targeting** doesn't
offer brush squares as dash destinations. **Fix: backlog BRUSH1** — verify/allow brush dash
targets in the client + add an engine regression asserting a dash can end in brush and gains
concealment.

### 4. [Low — coverage gap] RND1 rendering has no automated verification
The Builder flagged (DECISIONS) that `readPixels`/`drawImage` give all-black false negatives on
the WebGL canvas in the headless harness — only composited screenshots verify rendering. So the
3D renderer's *output* is unit-test-blind (its inputs — cues, view-model — are covered).
Acceptable for v1; a screenshot/composited smoke test is a deferred item when tooling allows.

### 5. [Nit] ARCHITECTURE.md is half-updated for RND1
The **Direction** section now describes the orthographic 3D renderer, but the **Stack** table
("SVG rendering") and the **Rendering (client)** section still describe SVG. Builder doc-fix.

No correctness defects in the shipped MET1/FF1/AIM2/RND1 game logic.
